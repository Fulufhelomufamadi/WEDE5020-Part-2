Welcome to Tshiawelo Primary School

Empowering Learners for a Brighter Future

Tshiawelo Primary School is a public primary school located in Tshitungulwane Village, Vuwani, Limpopo.The school serves learners from Grade R to Grade 7. The school provides quality education to learners from Grade R to Grade 7 in a supportive and respectful learning environment. Our goal is to help learners develop the knowledge, skills and values they need to succeed in their education and future lives.

Empowering learners to achieve excellence through quality education, discipline and respect!

About Tshiawelo Primary School

Providing Quality Education for Every Child

Tshiawelo Primary School is a public primary school located in Tshitungulwane Village, Vuwani, Limpopo. The school is committed to providing quality education in a safe, caring, and supportive environment where every learner is encouraged to reach their full potential. Our dedicated teachers work closely with parents and the community to promote academic excellence, discipline, respect, and teamwork. We believe that every child deserves an opportunity to succeed through education and positive values.

Our Vision

To provide quality education that develops responsible, confident, and successful learners who contribute positively to society.

Our Mission

• To provide quality teaching and learning.
• To encourage discipline and respect.
• To create a safe and supportive learning environment.
• To prepare learners for lifelong success.
